Core Architecture & Modality Trade-Offs

Native Multimodality (Early Fusion) & Sparse MoE

Google’s Gemini foundational models are built on native multimodality (early fusion). Instead of bolting separate vision or audio encoders onto a pretrained text-only LLM, all modalities — text, code, images, audio, video, and PDFs — project into a unified token space during pretraining and pass through a single Sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) transformer backbone. Conditional routing activates only a specialized subset of expert networks per token, decoupling massive total knowledge capacity from active per-token FLOPs and Time-to-First-Token (TTFT) (replacing legacy dense PaLM 2).

  • Why late-fusion cascades fail interactive UX: converting audio/video into text captions strips paralinguistic cues (tone, sarcasm, urgency) and spatial-temporal alignment, compounds serial latency (ASR → LLM → TTS), and causes text-dominant hallucinations when modalities conflict.

PAA Exam Trade-off — Native Multimodal vs. Modular Perception Pipelines

Choose native multimodal (gemini-3.8-live, gemini-4-argon GA agentic video understanding) when sub-second conversational latency, prosody/visual nuance, or dynamic video zooming is critical. Choose a modular perception pipeline (Chirp 3 / gemini-3.5-transcribe or Document AI Layout Parser → Sensitive Data Protection PII redaction → text LLM) when compliance requires auditable verbatim transcripts, deterministic PII scrubbing before reasoning, custom domain phrase adaptation, or caching extracted text across many downstream passes.

Starting with Gemini 4, Google transitioned frontier model naming to periodic-table elements, led by Gemini 4 Argon (gemini-4-argon, GA October 13, 2026) and Fairwind-allowlisted Gemini 4 Argon Cyber Permissive (gemini-4-argon-cyber-permissive) (note: periodic naming starts with Argon — there is no "Gemini 4 Carbon" model).

Client-side inference under strict mobile/browser thermal, battery, and RAM limits (Android AICore, Chrome Built-in AI, LiteRT-LM) → zero cloud cost, offline resilience, and data never leaves the device.
Flash-Lite (Gemini 3.5 / 3.1 Flash-Lite)
Ultra-low-cost, highest-QPS cloud tier (gemini-3.5-flash-lite) engineered for front-door intent routing, inline guardrails, entity extraction, classification, and bulk enrichment.
Flash Workhorse (Gemini 3.8 Flash & 3.8 Flash Cyber)
Flagship agentic loop workhorse (gemini-3.8-flash, plus 3.7 / 3.6 / 3.5 Flash) trained via knowledge distillation for long-horizon coding, rapid MCP tool calling, and default CodeMender execution.
Live, Speech & TTS (Gemini 3.8 Live / TTS / 3.5 Transcribe)
gemini-3.8-live & gemini-3.8-live-extended-thinking for sub-second voice/video agents; gemini-3.8-flash-tts (130 languages) & flash-lite-tts; gemini-3.5-transcribe & 3.5-live-translate-preview.
Frontier Reasoning (Gemini 4 Argon & 3.1 Pro Preview)
gemini-4-argon delivers peak System-2 reasoning across unified 1M context, long decodes up to 1M tokens, GA agentic video understanding, computer use, formal math/science, legal/finance, and defensive cyber.
Managed Agent Endpoints (Antigravity & Deep Research)
Task-level autonomous agents — antigravity-preview-09-2026 (isolated Linux coding/browsing sandbox), deep-research-preview-04-2026 / deep-research-max, and CodeMender.

Lifecycle status: Gemini 2.5 (2.5 Pro, 2.5 Flash, 2.5 Flash-Lite) remains in legacy support for existing workloads and managed SFT/preference tuning on Agent Platform, while new projects target 3.5 Flash-Lite, 3.8 Flash, and 4 Argon. Gemini 2.0 Flash / Flash-Lite, gemini-2.5-computer-use-preview-10-2025, and Gemini Robotics ER 1.6 are shut down.

Agentic Model Routing & Selection Framework

Golden rule: pick the smallest, fastest, cheapest model that passes your golden evaluation set, and route dynamically across tiers per agent step rather than sending every turn to a frontier model.

Multi-Model Agentic Cascade & Vertex AI Model Optimizer

Use Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (or ShieldGemma 2) at the front door for sub-200ms intent routing, slot extraction, and guardrails → run your core MCP tool-calling and coding loops on Gemini 3.8 Flash → escalate only hard multi-step planning, conflict resolution, or repository synthesis to Gemini 4 Argon (or enable Vertex AI Model Optimizer to route automatically across Gemini tiers by cost/quality target).

LLM vs. SLM & Managed vs. Self-Hosted

  • LLM (4 Argon / 3.8 Flash) vs. SLM (Gemma 4 / Nano / 3.5 Flash-Lite): LLMs excel at zero-shot long-horizon planning and 1M context; SLMs win high-QPS narrow schemas, edge latency, and fully offline client privacy.
  • Managed API vs. Self-Hosted: Default to managed APIs for fastest time-to-market and bursty traffic; self-host only for custom weights, air-gapped/strict private data paths, or proven lower TCO at steady saturated GPU utilization.

Open-Weight vs. Proprietary Lifecycle

  • Proprietary (Gemini, Claude): Peak frontier reasoning and managed safety, but subject to vendor retirement timelines (pin stable IDs, never -latest; retired IDs return 404).
  • Open-weight (Gemma 4, Llama): Permanent weight control and zero forced retirement, but you own patching, scaling, and safety guardrails (wrap with Model Armor + ShieldGemma 2). Remember: open models can also be consumed serverless via Model Garden MaaS!

Gemini 4 Argon, Gemini 3.x & Context Mechanics

Gemini 4 Argon (gemini-4-argon) Architecture & Strict Constraints

  • Unified 1M Token Budget (1,048,576 tokens): Shares a single 1M-token pool across input prompt + internal thinking + function calls + generated output.
  • Long Decodes up to 1M Tokens: Default single sync response cap is 65,536 tokens (32,768 non-streaming HTTP), extendable up to the full 1,048,576 budget (minus input) via continuation_token on GenerateContent (v1beta1) or server-side background=true on the Interactions API.
  • Thinking Levels (LOW | MEDIUM | HIGH): Defaults to MEDIUM. Passing legacy integer thinking_budget OR thinking_level="MINIMAL" on gemini-4-argon returns HTTP 400 INVALID_ARGUMENT! (Note: MINIMAL is also rejected on gemini-3.8-flash and gemini-3.7-flash, but is supported on gemini-3.6-flash and gemini-3.5-flash*; integer thinking_budget belongs to Gemini 2.5, where 2.5 Pro minimum is 128 tokens.)
  • Sampling Parameters Ignored: Custom temperature, top_p, and top_k values are ignored on gemini-4-argon.
  • Implicit Caching Only & Feature Matrix: Uses automatic durable implicit caching (4,096-token minimum, zero storage fee, up to 95% discount on Gemini API). Explicit cachedContents, Managed Tuning, Live API WebSockets, and direct RAG Engine generator binding are NOT supported on gemini-4-argon. Supports GA Agentic Video Understanding, Computer Use, Code Execution, MCP/Function Calling, and Grounding.
  • Gemini 4 Argon Cyber Permissive (gemini-4-argon-cyber-permissive): Allowlisted via DeepMind's Fairwind Program (global & us) for defensive vulnerability research, reverse engineering, and CodeMender; defaults to HIGH thinking and disables Computer Use.

GenerateContent vs. Interactions API & Caching

  • GenerateContent: Stateless client-managed turns (pass history + opaque Thought Signatures across tool loops; enable include_thoughts=True for UI summaries).
  • Interactions API (client.interactions.create, Preview): Optional server-side state via previous_interaction_id + background=true for long-horizon decodes and Managed Agents.
  • Explicit Caching (cachedContents): Available on Gemini 2.5 and supported 3.x base models (60-min default TTL + hourly storage fee); disable all caching when strict Zero Data Retention (ZDR) applies.

Multimodal Tokenization Mechanics

  • Text: 256,000-token SentencePiece vocabulary.
  • Images: Dynamic 768×768 pixel tiling at 258 tokens per tile (plus configurable resolution tiers).
  • Video: ~263 tokens/sec at 1 FPS standard resolution (compressible to ~66–70 tokens/sec for multi-hour recordings), enhanced by GA agentic zoom/re-sampling.
  • Audio: Fixed compact 32 tokens per second interleaved with timestamp markers.

Generative Media Models (Images, Video & Music)

Nano Banana 2.1 / Pro & Imagen 3 / 4

  • Nano Banana 2.1 (gemini-nano-banana-2.1, GA + Priority PayGo): Flagship native Gemini image generator & mask-free multi-turn conversational editor (replaces retired standalone Imagen 4 on the developer Gemini API).
  • Nano Banana 2 (gemini-3.1-flash-image), 2 Lite (gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image) & Pro (gemini-3-pro-image): 131,072-token context, up to 4K resolution, Google Search-grounded typography/infographics, and up to 14 reference images on Pro (5 character + 6 object + 3 style) for multi-turn identity preservation.
  • Imagen 3 & Imagen 4 (Agent Platform): Specialized latent-diffusion text-to-image endpoints (480-token prompt, up to 2K) with built-in SynthID and C2PA Content Credentials.

Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash & Veo 3.1

  • Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash (gemini-omni-1.1-flash): Fast native multimodal video generation, instruction-based video editing, keyframe interpolation, and clip extension with native audio.
  • Veo 3.1 (Generate, Fast, Lite — GA on Agent Platform): Joint latent diffusion synthesizing 4K 16:9 / 9:16 video and synchronized lip-sync/Foley audio in one shared latent space with strong object permanence (succeeding silent Veo 2).

Lyria 3.5, Lyria 3 & RealTime

  • Temporal Latent Diffusion: Denoises compact 44.1–48 kHz stereo waveform latents without autoregressive drift.
  • Lineup: Lyria 3.5 (lyria-3.5) for full-length structurally coherent songs, Lyria 3 Pro (184s), Lyria 3 Clip (30s), Lyria RealTime (lyria-realtime-exp), and Lyria 2 — accepting text/image prompts, BPM/structure controls, and multilingual vocals with SynthID-Audio watermarking.

Live API, 3.8 TTS, 3.5 Transcribe, Chirp 3 & Embedding 2

Gemini 3.8 Live & Native Speech Models

  • Gemini 3.8 Live (gemini-3.8-live): Default stateful WebRTC/WebSocket native speech-to-speech & video agent (16-bit PCM in → 24 kHz audio out) with server-side VAD barge-in, affective dialogue, and spoken function calling (71.5%+ ComplexFuncBench Audio).
  • Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking (gemini-3.8-live-extended-thinking): Runs deep background reasoning asynchronously without stalling live spoken conversation.
  • Dedicated TTS, STT & Live Translate: Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (130 languages, studio voice design) & Flash-Lite TTS (101 languages); Gemini 3.5 Transcribe (low-latency diarized STT with vocabulary biasing); Gemini 3.5 Live Translate (70+ language prosody-preserving S2S).

Chirp 3 (Speech-to-Text V2) & Gemini Embedding 2

  • Chirp 3 (chirp_3): Built on the 2B+ parameter Universal Speech Model (USM) (12M+ hours, 100+ languages) for compliance ASR pipelines requiring StreamingRecognize / BatchRecognize, Model Adaptation (up to 1,000 custom phrases), denoise_audio=True, and word-level timestamps.
  • Gemini Embedding 2 (gemini-embedding-2) & gemini-embedding-001: gemini-embedding-2 is Google's first native multimodal Gemini embedding model, mapping text, code, images, audio, video, and PDFs into one shared space. Both use Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL) for truncatable L2-normalized dimensions (e.g., 3,072 → 768 / 256).

Gemma 4 & Open-Weight Edge Models

Gemma 4, Gemma 3n & Gemma 3 Architecture

  • Gemma 4 (E2B, E4B, 26B A4B MoE, 31B): Flagship open-weight family combining mobile-first Per-Layer Embeddings (PLE) on E2B/E4B (processing text, image, video, and native audio in a 2B–4B RAM envelope), an ultra-fast 26B Sparse MoE activating only 4B parameters per token (26B A4B) for self-hosted agentic loops, and a dense 31B checkpoint.
  • Gemma 3 (1B–27B) & Gemma 3n: Introduced 128K context, 140+ languages, frozen 400M SigLIP vision encoding (256 soft tokens per 896×896 Pan & Scan crop), 5:1 local-to-global interleaved attention (5 sliding-window 1,024-token layers per 1 global layer to shrink KV-cache RAM), and MatFormer parameter sharing.
  • T5Gemma & RecurrentGemma (Griffin): Open encoder-decoder checkpoints (T5Gemma) and hybrid Real-Gated Linear Recurrence (RG-LRU) models achieving constant fixed-size state memory on long edge streams.

Specialized Gemma Variants

  • ShieldGemma 2 (4B) & ShieldGemma (2B, 9B, 27B): Open text and image safety classifiers scoring prompts, completions, and synthetic/natural images against harm policies.
  • MedGemma (4B, 27B), MedSigLIP (800M) & TxGemma (2B–27B): Clinical text/radiology/pathology multimodal models (replacing legacy standalone MedLM alongside Gemini) and therapeutic small-molecule/biologic discovery models.
  • PaliGemma 2 (3B–28B), CodeGemma (2B, 7B) & FunctionGemma: Fine-tunable VLMs (detection/segmentation), IDE Fill-in-the-Middle (FIM) completion, and compact tool-calling adapters.

Safety, SynthID & Frontier Governance

SynthID Imperceptible Watermarking

Paired with C2PA Content Credentials, SynthID embeds imperceptible statistical/cryptographic signatures directly into generated payloads so provenance survives metadata stripping:

  • SynthID-Text: Seeded cryptographic g-function tournament sampling skewing token choices without hurting fluency or perplexity.
  • SynthID-Audio: Psychoacoustic spectral embedding surviving MP3 compression and the "analog hole" (speaker playback re-recorded by mic).
  • SynthID-Image & Video: Spatial-temporal latent perturbations resilient to cropping, filters, and transcoding.

Frontier Safety Framework (FSF) & Model Armor

  • Critical Capability Levels (CCLs): Pre-deployment gating across CBRN uplift, Autonomous Cyber-Offense (why gemini-4-argon-cyber-permissive requires Fairwind Program vetting with Computer Use disabled), Autonomous ML R&D, and Deceptive Alignment.
  • Mitigation Pillars: Security Mitigations (weight exfiltration hardening) + Deployment Mitigations (safety tuning, runtime sentinels, gated access).
  • Runtime Guardrails: Per-request SafetySetting thresholds + Vertex AI Model Armor + ShieldGemma 2.

Scientific & Agentic Discovery Models

Agentic Discovery: AI Co-Scientist, AlphaEvolve & CodeMender

  • AI Co-Scientist: Multi-agent research tournament built on Gemini (Generate → Reflect → Rank → Evolve → Proximity → Meta-review) using Elo-rated self-play to synthesize experimentally validated biomedical hypotheses.
  • AlphaEvolve: Evolutionary coding agent pairing Gemini synthesis with deterministic automated evaluators to discover novel provably correct matrix-multiplication algorithms, math bounds, and Borg/TPU kernel optimizations.
  • CodeMender: Autonomous defensive cyber agent powered by gemini-3.8-flash and gemini-4-argon (cm find / verify / fix) for vulnerability discovery, sanitizer verification, and automated patching.

AlphaFold 3, AlphaProteo & AlphaGeometry 2

  • AlphaFold 3: Replaces Evoformer with a 48-block Pairformer, atomic-level tokenization for ligands/ions, and a 3D Conditional Diffusion Module across proteins, DNA, RNA, ligands, and ions.
  • AlphaProteo: De novo generative design of high-affinity protein binders (3×–300× affinity improvement).
  • AlphaGeometry 2: Solves 84% of IMO geometry problems via a neuro-symbolic loop pairing a Gemini auxiliary-construct generator with the infallible DDAR symbolic engine.
Gemini Robotics ER 2

Genie 2 / 3 & SIMA 2

AlphaMissense & DolphinGemma

GraphCast & GenCast

AlphaTensor, AlphaDev & AlphaCode 2
